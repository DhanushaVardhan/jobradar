      How it works

      1. CollectTwice a day, a GitHub Actions job reads the public job-board APIs that companies use for their own careers pages (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters), plus remote-job sites (Himalayas, Remotive, Remote OK, Jobicy) and Adzuna India.
      2. CleanJobs are filtered to India and India-friendly remote roles, de-duplicated across sources, and closed when they disappear from the company's board.
      3. UnderstandA free LLM (Groq, with Gemini as backup) reads each new posting and extracts a one-line summary, required and nice-to-have skills, years of experience, and whether freshers can apply. A rule-based parser covers anything the free quota can't.
      4. Match & publishThis static site, RSS feeds and Telegram posts are generated from the results. Matching against your resume happens in your browser.

      Every job links to its original posting. Jobs from aggregators are credited to them ("via Himalayas" and so on), as their API terms require.
